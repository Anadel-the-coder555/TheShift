          Before you go any further…

          Playing The Shift may cause you to be warped across space, time and at least one garden. Travelers usually come out the other side about half alive, fully covered in fur, and wearing a hat they don't remember buying.

          Possible side effects include:

          • Whiskers
          • An overwhelming urge to collect seeds
          • Hopping when excited
          • The hat. The hat does not come off. We've tried.

          The Shift is not responsible for lost thumbs, found tails, or becoming emotionally attached to a raccoon bandit.
